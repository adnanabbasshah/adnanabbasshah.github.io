Between 1 January and 20 February 2026, more than 56% of South Asia received below-normal
precipitation. Over a third of the region recorded its lowest wet-day count in the 1979-2026 ERA5
record.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/blog_dryness.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

The concern is sequencing. A dry winter depletes soil moisture going into the pre-monsoon window,
which removes the evaporative buffer that normally damps early heat. Dry winters and severe
pre-monsoon heat stress are not independent events in this region.
