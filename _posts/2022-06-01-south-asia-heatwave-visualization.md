An animation of the 2022 pre-monsoon heatwave over Pakistan and India, built from ERA5 850 hPa
temperature anomalies with 500 hPa geopotential height contours overlaid.

{% include video.liquid path="assets/video/heatwave.mp4" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" controls=true %}

The geopotential contours do most of the explanatory work here. The anomaly field alone shows that it
was hot; the ridge shows why the heat persisted rather than dispersing.
