A March 2026 heat dome pushed Arizona more than 10 °C above the 1991-2020 baseline.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/blog_heatdome.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

ERA5 anomaly analysis places this among the most extreme warm events on record for the region at
this point in the calendar. What makes early-season events like this consequential is not the peak
value alone but the timing: it arrives before vegetation, soil moisture and water management systems
are seasonally prepared for it.
