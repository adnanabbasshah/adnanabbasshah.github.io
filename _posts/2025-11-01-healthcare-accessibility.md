This map shows the share of population able to reach a primary healthcare facility within 10 to 60
minutes. Darker blues mark stronger accessibility, lighter yellows mark gaps.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/blog_health.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

- Balochistan has the lowest accessibility nationwide, driven by distance and sparse connectivity.
- Southern Punjab and Upper Sindh show notable gaps within the first 30 minutes.
- Mountainous terrain in KP and Gilgit-Baltistan extends travel times independent of facility density.
- Central Punjab and the major cities benefit from denser facilities and better road networks.

These patterns point to where investment in mobility, facility siting and emergency response would
change outcomes most.
