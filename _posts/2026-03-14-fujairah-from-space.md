At 04:00 UTC on 14 March 2026, Meteosat imagery captured smoke rising over Port of Fujairah after a
reported drone attack.

Ground access in situations like this is limited and contested. Satellites are not. Thick smoke
plumes and thermal signatures were resolvable from roughly 36,000 km above the surface, giving an
independent record of the event as it developed.

This is the part of remote sensing that rarely makes it into methods sections: observing fires,
tracking impacts and documenting events when nothing else can get close.
