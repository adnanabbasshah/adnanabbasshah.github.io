Sustainable Environmental Management (SEM) Pvt. Ltd.

predictive modelling, time series analysis and spatiotemporal anomaly detection. Delivered with
Google Earth Engine, Python, JavaScript (Leaflet, Mapbox) and PostgreSQL/PostGIS.

Over 30 projects delivered for clients across eight countries.
