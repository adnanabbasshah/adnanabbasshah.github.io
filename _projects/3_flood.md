Flood susceptibility mapping for District Jhang using six machine learning models: RNN, ANN, Random
Forest, MLP, SVM and KNN. Conditioning factors were derived from Landsat, Sentinel-2, SRTM, CHIRPS
and Dynamic World.

KNN and RNN performed best, both reaching AUC = 0.99. Between 27.2% and 39% of the study area falls
into high or very high susceptibility classes depending on the model. A Google Earth Engine web app
accompanies the paper for interactive inspection of conditioning factors and the final susceptibility
surfaces.

Published at IEEE FIT 2024.
