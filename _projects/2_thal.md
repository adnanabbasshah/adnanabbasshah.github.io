Arid land research has been split between narratives of advancing desertification and reports of
anomalous greening. This study tested both against 34 years of observations in the Thal Desert,
Pakistan.

I processed 1,620 scenes from Landsat 5 TM and Landsat 8 Collection 2 OLI, alongside MODIS land
surface temperature and CHIRPS precipitation, and tracked NDVI and EVI from 1990 to 2024.

**Findings.** Roughly 81% of the desert area shifted toward vegetated cover. Surface temperature rose
2.20 °C per decade, evapotranspiration increased 18.02 mm per decade, and precipitation increased
47.28 mm per decade. The greening is real, but it sits on top of a warming and drying-risk signal
rather than replacing it.
