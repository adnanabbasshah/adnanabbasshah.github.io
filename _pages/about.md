I am Adnan Abbas Shah, from Jhang, currently based in Islamabad. I work as GIS Consultant at Sustainable Environmental Management (SEM) Pvt. Ltd., Islamabad, and conduct research at the Environmental and Air Quality Research Lab, Department of Meteorology, COMSATS University Islamabad.

At SEM I lead geospatial project delivery, environmental impact assessment support, Web GIS development, and client advisory on remote sensing solutions, having progressed from GIS Analyst to Specialist to Consultant since 2023.

My research concerns hydroclimatic extremes in South Asia: how drought, heat, and land cover change interact, and what those interactions imply for water and food security in Pakistan. Recent work includes entropy-based drought characterization in the Jhelum River Basin, compound drought and heatwave events over Pakistan under CMIP6 scenarios, cropland dynamics and food security across Punjab, and marine heatwaves in the North Indian Ocean. Methodologically I work with cloud-based Earth observation, spatial machine learning, and reanalysis products, primarily Google Earth Engine, Python, and R applied to Landsat, Sentinel, MODIS, ERA5-Land, and CHIRPS archives.

I received my MS in Remote Sensing and GIS from COMSATS University Islamabad in 2025 and my BS in Geography from Government College University Faisalabad in 2022. My MS research quantified agricultural land conversion under rapid urbanization across Punjab using multi-decadal satellite archives and machine learning classification.

Independently I have completed over thirty freelance and consulting projects for research groups, graduate students, and organizations across eight countries, covering flood and drought hazard mapping, land cover analysis, climate data processing, and spatial modelling.

I am seeking a PhD position in Earth observation, hydroclimatology, or GeoAI.
